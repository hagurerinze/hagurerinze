## About math

I am not good at math. I also do not like it. I really, really do not like it.

Math is outside my passion. I put it in the group of things I want to avoid.

### Why

- Since I was a kid (even in kindergarten), my brain is not good at memorizing. It is still like this now.
- In junior and senior high school, I was not interested in school. Especially math, and anything I must memorize like math.
- I have little experience in math, only since college. It does not click fast for me. I am very slow at it, like a turtle.

### But

I still practice when I need it for quizzes, tasks, and exams. For example, I practiced row operations on matrices for a quiz. My method was right. My mistakes were small: signs, and forgetting the last number in a row. I had the same problem in semester 1 and 2.

I am more interested in computers, operating systems, and low-level things.
